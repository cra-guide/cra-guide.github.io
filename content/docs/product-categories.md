Some products with digital elements should undergo stricter conformity
assessment procedures.
Those products are referred to as important products with digital elements.
And are products deemed to carry higher cybersecurity risks.
Those products are further divided into two classes based on risk level.
An incident for class II products might have greater negative impact than class
I.
Because of this, products in class II are subject to mandatory third-party
conformity assessment.

Last, there are critical products with digital elements.
These are subject to the most strict assessment criteria.
