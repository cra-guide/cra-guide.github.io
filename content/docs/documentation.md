Compliance with CRA requires a couple of different kinds of documentation.
The **technical documentation** as defined in [ANNEX
VII](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202402847#anx_VII).
Then there are the **declaration of conformity**, either EU Declaration of
Conformity defined in [ANNEX
V](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202402847#anx_V)
or simplified declaration of conformity defined in [ANNEX
VI](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202402847#anx_VI).
Last, there is **information and instructions to the user** as defined in [ANNEX
II](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202402847#anx_II).

# Technical documentation

The technical documentation isn't required to be public, except some case for open source products (see [conformity assessment](./conformity-assessment.md)).
It shall however be kept at disposal of the market surveillance authorities together with the EU declaration of conformity, for at least 10 years or the support period, whichever is longer.
Depending on the [conformity assessment](./conformity-assessment.md) procedure chosen, it also needs to be available for notified body/conformity assessment body.
Therefore, it needs to be writing so it is understandable by those authorities.
Meaning it must be comprehensive, clear and in a language that can easily be
understood.
Requirements for the technical documentation are [article 31](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202402847#art_31) and [ANNEX VII](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202402847#anx_VII).

Note, determination of the support period shall be based on the criteria stated
in [article 13,
8.](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202402847#art_13)

# Declaration of conformity

The declaration of conformity is a document in which the manufacturer declares
compliance and assumes responsibility for the obligations of CRA for product.
There are two types of declaration of conformity, EU declaration of conformity
([ANNEX
V](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202402847#anx_V))
and simplified declaration of conformity ([ANNEX
VI](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202402847#anx_VI)).

There are a couple of different ways conformity assessment can be carried out
as defined in [ANNEX
VIII](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202402847#anx_VIII).
Which [conformity assessment procedure](./conformity-assessment.md) that can be
applied, depend on [product category](./product-categories.md).
For the majority of product, self-assessment is enough.
However, it is important that the assessment is properly documented, as it is
the manufactures' responsibility to ensure compliance.

Prior to assessment, the manufacture must have implemented appropriate
mitigations based on the [risk assessment](./risk-assessment.md).
The manufacture shall verify that the product comply with the essential
cybersecurity requirements in [ANNEX
I](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202402847#anx_I).
The methodology for verification (including testing) needs to be documented.
CRA doesn't dictate following a specific methodology.
Though, when the [harmonized standards](./standards.md) arrive, it will
probably set a de facto methodology.

It is important to note that a substantial modification to the product requires
re-assessment.
This includes changes to services around the product if the modification alters
the risk profile seen from the consumers point-of-view.

You might want to read section 6 of [FAQs on the Cyber Resilience
Act](https://ec.europa.eu/newsroom/dae/redirection/document/122331).

# User information

Products with digital elements must be accompanied "information and instructions to the user" as described in [ANNEX II](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202402847#anx_II).
It must be kept at disposal of users and authorities for at least 10 years or
the support period, whichever is longer.
It must be provided in a language which can be easily understood, intelligible
and legible to users and market surveillance authorities.
The information and instructions must include information on manufacture,
information that uniquely identify the product, single point of contact for reporting of vulnerabilities, end date for support, instructions on secure installation and operation of the product.

# Coordinated vulnerability disclosure (CVD) policy

Manufactures must put in place and enforce a policy on coordinated
vulnerability disclosure.
A reference to which must be included in the documentation.
